# Batchloom visual direction

Independent risograph print studio; a sophisticated maker's desk with real working controls. Warm bone paper (#f5f2e9), deep midnight ink (#20241f), cobalt (#334fc0), vermilion proof marks (#c35c3e), muted sage. Crisp editorial typography, deliberate asymmetric layouts, generous whitespace, fine ruled lines, tactile close-up imagery. Avoid generic finance dashboards, glowing gradients, excessive rounded cards, or invented testimonial/traction numbers.

Generated asset family: studio collection hero, cobalt notebook product, sage notebook alternative, warm die-cut button surface, cobalt action-button surface, stamp/action glyphs, success/recovery still life. Text, dollar amounts, statuses and receipts remain live HTML, never baked into decorative imagery. All meaningful buttons have an image-generated skin or generated pictogram plus clear focus/hover/disabled behavior. PayPal's branded button follows its SDK brand requirements, with the surrounding UI themed.

Hero concept: overhead editorial still life of bespoke cobalt botanical risograph notebooks, uncoated ivory sheets, terracotta proofing slip, linen ribbon and brass clip on a warm paper maker's desk. Handmade but precise. No readable labels/logos. Wide composition, no fake UI.

All images are fictional generated demonstration assets; no claim of real inventory or customer work. Keep prompts and tool provenance in this folder.
